## Paired compression comparison

The same deterministic payloads were written at levels **6, 9, 9, 6**, giving
two samples per level with reversed order in the second pair.

| Input component | Members | Uncompressed bytes |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Synthetic metadata | 1 | 32 |
| Opaque snapshot payload; no SQLite operation | 1 | 4,096 |
| Repeated 512-byte synthetic message payload | 95,423 | 48,856,576 |
| Mixed-entropy historical recovery payload | 1 | 16,777,216 |
| **Total** | **95,426** | **65,637,920** |

The recovery payload repeated a deterministic 1-MiB block 16 times. In each
4-KiB region, 1 KiB contained xorshift-generated bytes and 3 KiB contained zeros.
The generator used seed `0x4d595df4d0f33173` and shifts 13, 7, and 17.
ZIP input, member order, timestamps, buffer size, and fixture data were identical across runs.
Timing covered ZIP writing, finishing, flushing, and file synchronization.
Every member's name, size, and bytes were verified outside the measured interval.
The entire comparison passed in 95.43 seconds.

| Execution order | Compression level | Measured elapsed seconds | ZIP bytes | Contents verified |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 6 | 20.437735 | 39,008,712 | All members |
| 2 | 9 | 22.639758 | 38,818,370 | All members |
| 3 | 9 | 22.450287 | 38,818,370 | All members |
| 4 | 6 | 18.762374 | 39,008,712 | All members |

The two-sample medians were **19.6000545 seconds at level 6** and
**22.5450225 seconds at level 9**: level 6 had **13.06% shorter elapsed time**
and produced **190,342 more bytes (0.49034% larger)**.
This result applies only to this small, unoptimized-build ZIP fixture on a RAM
filesystem, with two samples per level and possible shared-host contention.
It is not an end-to-end archive speedup, a release-build estimate, an explanation
of the reported timeout, or evidence of a production backup completion guarantee.
